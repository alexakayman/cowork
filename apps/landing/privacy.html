Privacy

Cowork is designed to be minimal and respectful of your privacy:

  • No keylogging or history. We don't track keystrokes, save browsing history, or capture what you type. We only detect which app is in the foreground (e.g. "VS Code", "Chrome") — never specific URLs or page titles.
  • Only your session can see you. Your activity is streamed in real time only to friends in the same session. Nothing is stored on our servers; when the session ends, it's gone.
  • No account required. You pick a display name and avatar per session. We don't collect email, passwords, or identity.
  • Open source. The code is on GitHub so you can verify what the app does.
